# Parish reading room, subscribers' notice

The reading room is open on Thursday evenings and keeps novels and the county directory. It does not hold engineering drawings.

In 1904 the subscribers included Mrs Hale, who taught piano, and a gardener named Holne. Neither is described as a quarry, a surveyor, or a councillor. The leaky skylight was mended in 1902. The joiner's bill was £18. The notice says nothing about a beck, a weir, or a company ledger.
