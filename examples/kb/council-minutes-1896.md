# Borough of Calderbridge — minute, 12 March 1896

Present: the mayor, six aldermen, and Councillor Edmund Adey.

Greyfell Beck comes off the moor, passes the hamlet of Low Greyfell, and afterwards reaches the town weir. Adey moved that the council obtain a reservoir sufficient for a dry August. The motion carried.

Miss Helena Voss, of the firm Voss & Keene, is appointed engineer for a reservoir at the upper intake marked on the surveyor's sketch. No scheme is to be accepted if her estimate exceeds a ceiling of £48,000. The council may later subscribe for shares in a waterworks company; any such subscription is to be fixed by a subsequent minute. If filtration is wanted, it is a town work and is outside this appointment.

This minute fixes neither the height of a dam, nor a quarry, nor the day of first supply.
