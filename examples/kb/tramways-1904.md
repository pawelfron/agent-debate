# Inspector's card, Calderbridge electric tramways

Traffic began on 4 June 1904. The depot is on Mill Lane, inside the town, and it draws its water from the town main. The cars do not run to Low Greyfell or to the upper intake.

Edmund Adey chaired the tram committee while he lived. The card summarises that committee: fares, poles, and the Mill Lane shed. It says nothing about the reservoir.

A fitter named Keene oiled the cars. The card says no relationship is recorded between him and the firm Voss & Keene, which held no tramway contract.
