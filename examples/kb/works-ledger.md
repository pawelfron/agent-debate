# Calderbridge Waterworks Company — payments abstract

Kept by the company clerk. This is not a borough cash book. No line is headed with the borough treasurer, and the abstract does not state what share capital the borough eventually took.

- 2 July 1897. Contract with M. Pell & Sons. Sum reserved, £44,750.
- 1897 to 1899. Holne quarry, stone and haulage. Charged inside the contract, £3,860. Not an extra.
- 11 August 1898. One Pethwick sample to the yard. Labelled not for the dam. Petty account, £18. Not certified as dam stone.
- 20 October 1899. Resident clerk certifies practical completion. The same hand counts the certificate as twenty complete days after 30 September 1899.
- 11 December 1899. Last instalment to M. Pell & Sons. The contract sum of £44,750 is cleared in full.
- 9 January 1900. Liquidated damages enforced, described as part only of twenty days at £15: £210. The company waives the remainder. The council's representatives assent. The waiver is not a payment, and it is not taken out of the £44,750.
