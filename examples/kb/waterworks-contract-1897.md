# Articles of agreement, 2 July 1897

The employer is the Calderbridge Waterworks Company. The Borough Council is not the employer. The council is referred to only as a possible shareholder. Any share subscription sits outside these articles and is not priced here.

The contractor is M. Pell & Sons. The principal of that firm is Miriam Pell. Jonas Pell is not a party, and the articles do not appoint him.

The firm will build the earth dam, the spillway, and the pipe to the town weir, following the drawings of Helena Voss dated 3 April 1897. Holne stone is required for the pitching and for the protection of the core. Pethwick stone is forbidden in the dam.

The contract sum is £44,750. It includes the weir pipe. It excludes a filtration house, a tank, and every sand bed.

The resident clerk is to certify practical completion. Liquidated damages of £15 are due for each complete day by which that certificate falls after 30 September 1899. Those damages sit outside the contract sum. The articles do not describe them as a deduction from £44,750.
