# Letter, Helena Voss to the secretary of the Waterworks Company, 19 March 1898

Holne quarry stood in flood for nine days in February. No building stone left the quarry between 4 February and 2 March 1898. Carters from Pethwick offered a cheap load. Voss refused it for the works. The foreman may keep a single sample in the yard if a label says it is not for the dam. It must not be tipped into the core.

The works are already some weeks behind. She still hopes the resident clerk can certify before the autumn of 1899. The sentence in the April report, about water at the weir in the summer of 1899, should not be copied into any prospectus. She has not been asked to design a filter. She has not inspected the timber shed the borough surveyor set beside the town weir, and she does not treat that shed as part of the dam.
